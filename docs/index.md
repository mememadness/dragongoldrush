## What is Dragon Gold Rush?

A match-3 puzzle game where every reward is real: prizes, credits, and the
game's own coin all live on the [Nervos CKB](https://www.nervos.org/)
blockchain. Play free in Story Mode, or compete on the monthly leaderboard
for on-chain prizes.
